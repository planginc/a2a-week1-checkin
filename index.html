Side note for Jodi

Jodi, total side note I keep forgetting to mention! I've heard you talk about NotebookLM and that we could probably be using it for something. I have my own version of NotebookLM that I got from one of my Skool communities, and it's super cool.

It does what Google's NotebookLM does: you add documents, links, PDFs or YouTube videos, then chat with them, and the answers come only from your sources. It can also turn them into an audio overview like a podcast, plus flashcards and study guides.

The big difference is that it has an API, so Claude can connect to it directly: create a notebook, add sources and ask it questions, all automatically. It can also plug into tools like n8n and Zapier. Google's version can't do any of that. Happy to show you!

In one sentence: every A2A student Pam is responsible for gets a helpful Week 1 email (office hours replays + where everything lives), and anyone with a phone gets a short text right behind it, so Pam has a two-way conversation with each student before the Week 4 UCL push.
